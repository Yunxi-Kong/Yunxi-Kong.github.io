### Generation Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original editorial illustration for an academic essay titled 'The Next Bottleneck'. Landscape 3:2, high-resolution bitmap. Depict a precise, human-scale thought experiment in an elegant cut-paper and ink architectural illustration: on the left, a workbench produces many folded paper plans and small architectural models; in the center, one attentive person studies a single unfolded plan; on the right, a partly built pedestrian bridge leads toward a small public square where other people deliberate around a table. Not dystopia, not a promotional image: convey that abundant proposals do not automatically become shared action. Restrained black ink linework with subtle paper texture, warm white background, teal structural elements, small coral and green details, plenty of negative space. Clear composition readable at small sizes, visually interesting close up. No words, letters, logos, data charts, robots, brains, glowing effects, gradients, decorative orbs, or corporate branding. This is a conceptual editorial artwork, not a photograph or empirical research figure.

## cognitive-exoskeleton

Assets: `public/assets/essays/cognitive-exoskeleton.webp` and `public/assets/essays/cognitive-exoskeleton-720.webp`.

### Generation Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original editorial bitmap illustration for an essay on personal cognitive infrastructure, landscape 3:2. A person at an open desk is composing an idea on a large sheet, surrounded by a small carefully organized arc of external tools: open books, a laptop displaying simple unlabelled branching possibilities, a phone, a watch, a small archive of notes, and three physical translucent-looking paper models of alternative designs. Each tool is connected by a fine visible ink line to a central handwritten plan the person is actively revising. The person, not the computer, holds the pencil and changes the direction of the plan. Connections are modest and finite, not a glowing technological web. View is elevated three-quarter, spacious, asymmetrical and intimate. Sophisticated ink drawing and printed-paper collage, crisp charcoal outlines, pale cool grey-white negative space, deep teal, coral red and moss green accents. An intellectually curious essay illustration, not SaaS marketing or a literal robotic exoskeleton. No text, logos, words, numbers, data charts, brain icons, robots, mechanical implants, sci-fi HUDs, gradients or glowing orbs. The idea is supported judgment, memory and action while a human retains authorship of goals.

## coordination

Assets: `public/assets/essays/coordination.webp` and `public/assets/essays/coordination-720.webp`.

### Generation Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Original editorial illustration, panoramic 2:1 bitmap for the middle of a long essay about AI agents and organizational coordination. An overhead architectural view of six separate small work tables arranged around an open shared central table. At the small tables are folded paper constructions, notebooks, tools, and a few human figures. Fine charcoal paths lead from each work table toward a shared plan in the center. Some routes overlap or double back, while two collaborators at the central table carefully reconcile different pieces into one coherent structure. The tension is many productive workers versus the work of coordinating them. Make it a sophisticated conceptual cut-paper print with clean white background, precise black ink hatching, vivid but restrained deep teal and vermilion details, touches of leaf green. No beige wash. Strong simple shapes with handcrafted detail, generous margins, no border. No letters, labels, logos, robot faces, brain symbols, numeric data, charts, sci-fi, gradients or glowing effects. Not a factual diagram or a company office photograph; this is a thoughtful, original visual metaphor.

## interactive-understanding

### Generation Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Original conceptual editorial bitmap illustration for a scholarly essay about learning through interactive environments rather than just receiving explanations. Panoramic 2:1. A tactile miniature landscape sits on a shared work table: hills, a river, a few small buildings, paths. One person's hands move a small removable bridge piece; nearby a second version of the same landscape shows a different route through it. Subtle charcoal line arrows show two alternative journeys, not numeric measurement. A simple open notebook has sketched alternatives but no words. The scene should make the act of changing a condition and inspecting its consequences immediately legible. Precise fine-ink architectural drawing mixed with textured cut-paper illustration, white background, teal water, forest-green terrain, restrained coral bridge, charcoal lines. Clear, elegant, grounded, thoughtful, roomy white margins, no border. Not a game screenshot or actual experiment. No text, labels, logos, floating user interfaces, robots, brains, glowing effects, gradients, or photographic faces.
